Plan

Day one

Drive in through the west entrance, hike Ryan Mountain before noon, sunset at Keys View.

Day two

Sunrise at Cholla Cactus Garden, then Skull Rock and the drive out through the south.

Packing

  • Four liters of water per person per day
  • Headlamp for the sunset walk back
  • Layers: nights drop below 5 °C in spring

Check road and trail status on the park website before leaving.
