Onboarding redesign — design review

Three-screen onboarding for the new workspace product. This page records the decisions and the open questions from the review on Thursday.

Flow agreed Copy pending Open question

Goals

  • Get a new team to its first shared workspace in under two minutes.
  • Never show a screen the user cannot skip or undo.
  • Say why each permission is asked for, in one sentence.

Proposed flow

1. Create workspace

Name + region. Region is pre-filled from the browser locale and can be changed later.

2. Invite team

Paste email addresses, or copy a join link. Skippable.

3. First document

Pick a template. This is the moment we want the user to feel the product, not a form.

The flow, as a diagram

The dashed part is what we would remove: the old “choose a plan” step between workspace and invite.

Open questions

  • Do we trust the browser locale for the region default, or ask explicitly?
  • Is the invite step worth its screen if most users skip it?
  • Should the first screen say “workspace” or “team space”?
